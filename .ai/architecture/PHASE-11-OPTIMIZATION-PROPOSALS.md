# Phase 11 offline optimization proposals — TASK-0065

The AI gateway of PHASE-10 produces only offline, budgeted proposals. This module accepts a typed candidate only with a separately verified gateway trace, prompt/context hashes, source IDs and actual spend bounded by a configured cost ceiling. There is deliberately no production `OptimizationReceiptVerifier`; an untrusted caller cannot provide its own pass flag to authorize a live proposal.

The proposal freezes the TASK-0064 binding hash, exact candidate matrix, source/context provenance, uncertainty, risk, budget and author. The deterministic evaluator is an actor distinct from the proposer; it verifies all content and audience references in the workspace, the original plan, snapshot/control and candidate arm names. High risk or high uncertainty leads to rejection. The human reviewer has both AI and campaign approval permissions and must differ from proposer and evaluator. Review is bound to exact proposal and evaluation hashes. It produces only a `reviewed_draft` receipt with `publication_authorized: false`; no campaign, experiment, audience, schedule, send or provider table is modified. Rollback marks that receipt reverted while retaining the candidate and review evidence.

The ledger does not perform model inference, budget reservation or statistical winner selection. Cost accounting belongs to the existing PHASE-10 gateway; the independent receipt verifier must prove it. Production adoption requires a trusted receipt implementation, actual data-quality and statistical gates from TASK-0066, and separate live activation authority. Synthetic test receipts demonstrate the state machine only.
