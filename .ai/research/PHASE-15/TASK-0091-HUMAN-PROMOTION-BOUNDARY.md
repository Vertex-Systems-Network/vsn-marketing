# TASK-0091 — Independent Offline Human Promotion Boundary

This is an **offline-only, non-authorizing** review. A canary statistical signal cannot promote itself, and a fabricated human sign-off cannot create production authority.

- The subject must be an exact plan + analysis + frozen-cohort receipt + independently linked outcome manifest. Any drift or unrecognized score field fails closed.
- A separately injected human decision source must certify the **latest** decision, authenticated independent actor and current workspace permission. The default source returns no decision; caller/model payloads never determine source authority.
- Decisions must bind experiment, plan hash, analysis hash, cohort receipt ID, outcome manifest SHA, policy version, and valid observation/expiry windows. Rejected, revoked, self-approved, untrusted and stale decisions cannot advance even an offline review.
- An exact positive human decision yields operator-readable offline evidence only. `promotion_authorized=false`, `execution_authorized=false`, and `external_outcome_proven=false` always.
- There is **no installed production provider adapter, immutable human decision writer for canaries, atomic action admission or external-effect verifier** in this slice. Do not classify it as real consent, spend permission, enrollment, successful promotion or completed TASK-0091 certification.
