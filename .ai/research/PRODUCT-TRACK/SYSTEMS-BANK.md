# Systems Bank — existing market systems (research 2026-10-17)

Purpose: catalogue the systems VSN Marketing competes with or integrates, what they do well, where users complain, and how VSN can be better. Input to every PRODUCT-TRACK phase research task (TASK-0102, 0108, 0116, 0122, 0126, 0130, 0138).

**Evidence quality:** gathered by AI web search on 2026-10-17. Most sources are 2026 comparison/affiliate blogs and vendor pages; prices and free-tier limits **conflict between sources** and must be re-verified on official pricing pages in each phase's research task before any product or pricing decision.

| System | Strengths | Pricing signal (unverified) | Weaknesses / user complaints |
|---|---|---|---|

## G. Market-wide pain points VSN should solve

1. Price jumps at contact thresholds and per-seat/per-channel pricing.
2. Email and social live in separate tools; users pay twice.
3. Open rates are unreliable since Apple Mail Privacy Protection (Apple estimated at ~49–62% of opens, conflicting sources); corporate scanners create fake clicks.
4. Free tiers shrinking (SendGrid) — users need provider choice and failover.
5. Cluttered interfaces, weak analytics, slow support, difficult cancellation.
6. AI features that are thin wrappers or credit-metered.
7. Agencies need white-label + client isolation (GoHighLevel's main advantage).
