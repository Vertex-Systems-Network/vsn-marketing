## Product requirements and evidence semantics

| ID | Feature | Target behavior | Non-negotiable reliability and privacy boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| EM-01 | Real-time read/open receipts | Authorized inbox and campaign UI can show an observed open with UTC timestamp and optional bounded in-app notification; label processing delay | Open pixels / provider open events are not proof of human reading; image blocking/proxies/prefetch may cause missed or false opens; no guaranteed instant or exact human-read claims |
| EM-02 | Device and browser | Show mobile/desktop/unknown and browser family only when verifiable metadata is available, otherwise unknown | Proxy/provider metadata can reflect an intermediary; never present inference as detected fact; avoid raw device fingerprints |
| EM-03 | Geographic tracking | Offer coarse country/region/city only where independently justified and policy permits, with source/confidence | Proxy/VPN/shared IP makes location approximate; no precise GPS or recipient home address inference; IP minimization and regional restrictions |
| EM-04 | Open count and timeline | Distinguish total observed open events, deduplicated source events, and approximate unique opens; show first/last/timezone/freshness | Retry/proxy effects, batch events, late data, multiple devices and ambiguous identities disclosed; count is not verified unique readers |
| EM-05 | Self-open / bot filtering | Exclude authorized sender test views, known QA opens and defensibly classified automated/proxy observations from human-engagement reporting; surface excluded/unknown counts | Do not silently erase raw event evidence, guess a user's IP, or claim all bot/prefetch events are identified; allow audit/reclassification |
| EM-06 | Link click tracking | Correlate consent-eligible link clicks to message/campaign and link ID, timeline and bounded engagement metrics; optionally report link-level totals | Signed opaque tokens, allowlisted destinations, redirect/SSRF/open-redirect protection, exclude unsubscribe/security links; clicks do not prove human intent or conversions |

Cross-cutting: delivered, bounced, complained, unsubscribed, blocked, unknown and unavailable statuses remain distinct; `send accepted` is not `delivered`, `opened` is not `read`, and `clicked` is not `converted`. Include provider-native event support tables with explicit unsupported status. Gmail delegated send-only is not evidence of recipient open callbacks.

## Candidate work packages and guarded dependency order

1. **TASK-0101 — Research and privacy/feasibility matrix.** Dated current official Brevo/SES/Gmail and selected provider documentation, Apple Mail Privacy Protection, mailbox image proxy behavior, GDPR/ePrivacy/Pakistan and target-market consent/retention requirements; competitor UX comparison only as research, not API authority. Produce provider capability/readiness matrix, collection lawful-basis policy, abuse threat model, and ADR proposal if architecture or privacy policy must materially change.
2. **TASK-0102 — Canonical tracking configuration and eligibility.** Versioned per workspace/brand/message/campaign tracking preferences; consent, purpose, privacy notice, retention, regional support, provider opt-out and tenant authorization; disabled/unknown fails closed. Ensure transactional/security email tracking does not inherit marketing consent. Deterministic effective-policy checks precede instrumenting or ingesting a recipient signal.
3. **TASK-0103 — Provider webhook and safe event ingestion.** Research-backed per-provider authenticators, replay/idempotency, scoped message association, `message.opened` / `message.clicked` canonical envelopes, provider/source reference hashing, late/out-of-order replay and quarantine. Unverified callbacks never become trusted analytics facts; unsupported provider paths show `unsupported`.
4. **TASK-0104 — Open instrumentation and signal quality.** Where permissible, secure unique pixel/tokens and/or provider-native open events; signed, expiring, minimally scoped tokens, image request abuse protection, retention and observed-vs-human-likelihood metadata; no promise of accurate read receipts. Self-test labeling and proxy/bot classification with explicit `unknown` and excluded counts.
5. **TASK-0105 — Safe click attribution and redirect service.** Signed per-message/link opaque reference, canonical destination validation, no arbitrary URL forwarding or open redirect/SSRF, safe handling of hostile URLs, click events from authorized signals, phishing/abuse protections, provider-native webhook comparison and exclusion of opt-out/security-action URLs from marketing engagement.
6. **TASK-0106 — Operator UX, reporting and notifications.** Message/campaign engagement timeline, count split and source-quality warnings, mobile/browser/coarse-geo where available, honest unknown/partial states, bounded event-to-notification latency SLO where measured, permission-checked real-time or near-real-time in-app notifications, accessibility/WCAG 2.2 AA, localized timezone, retention and erasure UX.
7. **TASK-0107 — Controlled AI use of engagement.** Registered explainable scoring and user-reviewed follow-up suggestions may consume verified, consent-eligible click events with bounded evidence/freshness; low-trust open or ambiguous bot signals must not independently trigger send, targeting, suppression override, auto-optimization, or person-level judgments. Existing PHASE-10/11/15 AI budget, approval, tool, kill-switch and canary gates stay authoritative. No provider sending permission is created by this track.
8. **TASK-0108 — Integration and certification.** PostgreSQL and Redis representative concurrent tests, integration contracts per supported provider, browser/E2E/a11y, security/tenant/privacy threat cases, spoofed/duplicate/late/out-of-order callbacks, token abuse/redirect fuzzing, Apple/proxy/blocked-image adverse fixtures, opt-out and deletion, disabled tracking negative cases, SLO measurements and exact-head/resulting-main CI. Record unsupported/unverified production claims separately. Only after passing applicable gates may the extension be certified.
